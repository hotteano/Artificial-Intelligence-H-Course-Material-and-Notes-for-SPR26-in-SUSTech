               Implementation Details
Ensemble critics. All M = 5 critics share the same MLP
architecture (two hidden layers of 256 units, ELU activa-
tions) but are initialized with different random seeds and up-
dated independently. No gradient sharing, parameter tying,
or diversification loss is applied.
Numerical stability. σc is clamped to σmin = 10−6 . The
ratio dκ = (µc − K)/σc is clipped to [−10, 10] before pass-
ing to Φ and ϕ to avoid numerical underflow.
PID controller. Anti-windup is implemented by freezing
the integral term when the projected dual variable is satu-
rated and the current error would drive it deeper into satura-
tion; when the error reverses, the integral term is allowed to
unwind. The default PID gains used in the experiments are
KP = 0.2, KI = 0.02, and KD = 0.05.
Cost limit calibration. All Clim values are specified in
per-step cost units and applied directly to the batch-mean
cost mean(ct ∈ Bk ) in the PID error signal, avoiding
episode-length dependence.
